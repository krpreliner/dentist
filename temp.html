Frequently Asked Questions

Is the treatment painful?

Not at all. We use state-of-the-art painless anesthesia techniques and advanced technology to ensure you are completely comfortable throughout your procedure.

How much does teeth whitening cost?

The cost varies depending on the specific treatment plan, but we offer affordable packages starting from ₹5,000. We also provide a free initial consultation to give you an exact estimate.

Do you offer dental implants?

Yes, Dr. Ruchi Jain is an implant specialist. We use premium, lifetime-guaranteed titanium implants that look and function exactly like natural teeth.

Do you provide emergency dental services?

Yes, we have a 24/7 priority emergency line for severe toothaches, trauma, or accidents. Call our clinic number immediately for urgent care.

Is my insurance accepted here?

We partner with most major health insurance providers. Our front desk will help you process your claims easily so you can focus on your recovery.
